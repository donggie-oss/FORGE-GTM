How Forge Works

Decide. Execute. Learn. Recalculate.

Forge starts from operational reality, separates evidence from assumption, and builds only what the commercial problem has earned.

01

Establish reality

What do we know? What do we not know? What is observation and what is inference? Where does the current commercial story contradict itself?

02

Decide what deserves effort

Pursue, hold, stop, research, reposition or change route. The objective is not to keep every idea alive.

03

Understand the buying reality

Who matters, what they care about, where authority sits, what blocks movement and what still needs to be learned.

04

Determine the next move

Every action needs a commercial job. The action follows the decision, not the other way around.

05

Execute with discipline

Communication, calls, research and meetings stay aligned to the current state rather than whatever sounds persuasive that day.

06

Capture outcome and recalculate

Sent is not an outcome. Called is not an outcome. What happened changes what happens next.

Build. Operate. Transfer.

Consulting should survive the consultant.

Build

Make the logic explicit.

Strategy, structure, decision rules, workflows and ownership.

Operate

Pressure-test it live.

Use real accounts, real buyers and real constraints. Find what breaks before handover.

Transfer

Move capability inside.

Documentation, operating rhythm and enough lived use that the team can run it without Forge.
