What accumulated

Experience became doctrine. Doctrine became systems.

The early lessons were simple: understand the process before the product. Stay when things break. Don’t confuse activity with progress. Walk away from bad-fit revenue. Those lessons became operating manuals, sales bibles, market-entry models, SignalGate, PANANAW and commercial execution systems.

See the operation

Protect the truth

A system that needs its creator to operate it isn’t finished. The work is preserving good commercial judgment without making every decision depend on one experienced person.

Where the work has led

SignalGate, PANANAW and commercial execution systems are not a departure from my career. They are the result of it.
