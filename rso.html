Recommended Solution Overview

The RSO is an output. Not the operating system.

An RSO turns verified understanding into something someone else can use to decide or act.

Where it belongs

Downstream of reality.

Before an RSO exists, the work should already understand enough of the commercial problem to separate what is known, what remains uncertain, what matters, what the evidence supports and what should happen next.

What an RSO can do

  • Articulate verified understanding.
  • Connect a problem to a defensible solution.
  • Align stakeholders around the same evidence.
  • Preserve decision logic across handoffs.
  • Support executive or commercial review.

The format follows the decision.

Not every decision needs eight pages. Some need one. Some need a few lines. The artifact should be only as large as the decision requires.
